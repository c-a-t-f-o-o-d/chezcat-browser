Use an Official branch / endorsement request issue in the official repository. Licensing, contractual notices and confidential commercial-permission enquiries may be directed to legal@bitmadd.com after its operation is verified; no email acknowledgement constitutes approval. This is a request channel only; opening an issue is not permission. A hosting request must separately identify whether anyone outside permitted Personal Use or Internal Use can operate the browser or consume its engine functionality, including invitation-only partner/vendor access and browser jobs delivered as a service.

The request should identify the applicant/maintainer, proposed variant, immutable source revision and modifications, test/security evidence, supported versions, requested branding, distribution channels, maintenance and security responsibilities, third-party components, and any payments, advertising, sponsorship, donations or other benefits connected to the proposal.

An approval must identify at least:

| Field | Required scope |
| --- | --- |
| Approver | Adam Maddox or a documented authorized representative/lawful successor |
| Recipient | Named legal entity/person and responsible maintainer |
| Material | Exact source revision, release and relevant artifact identities |
| Status | Unofficial, officially endorsed, suspended, expired or withdrawn |
| Rights granted | Branding, free distribution and/or hosted access, separately enumerated |
| Distribution | Approved source/binary versions and approved channels; no assumed future coverage |
| Hosting | Explicit approval, permitted audience and confirmation of no linked monetization |
| Quality control | Required test evidence, security standards, maintenance and review conditions |
| Duration | Start, end or review conditions; no implied perpetual endorsement |
| Remediation | Notice, correction, suspension and withdrawal rules stated in advance |
| Ownership | No implied transfer of base-project ownership or authority over future releases |
| Signature/record | Authenticated written grant with date and final text identity |

New approvals are discretionary as stated in LICENSE section 1.7, subject to its exceptions and applicable law. An approval issued under CSAPUL 1.1 must state any departure from that section's default: ordinary prospective withdrawal on at least 30 days' written notice after established receipt, and immediate written suspension where reasonably necessary for material breach, material misrepresentation or a serious security/misleading-endorsement risk. Identify affected rights, reason and effective time. An express separate agreement controls its own commitments. These rules do not retroactively amend older approvals or revoke compliant recipients' existing base-license grants. Expiry, correction and breach consequences remain as stated in the applicable terms.

An approved branch may use a designation such as “ChezCat [Variant] — Officially Endorsed Branch,” with its maintainer and original project attribution. Approval does not give away ownership, authorize sales, waive CSAPUL generally, or cover any future revision automatically.

## Approval register

The public register is [ENDORSED_BUILDS.md](../ENDORSED_BUILDS.md).

Record approval identifier, variant/maintainer, covered revisions, authorized channels, approved rights, period and current status. Do not populate the register with fabricated approvals or imply that any third-party branch is currently endorsed. Keep superseded entries as history and distinguish historical endorsement from current endorsement.

## Nonmonetized hosted access

Any external Hosted Access needs express approval, including genuinely free public, customer, invitation-only, partner or vendor access. Under the agreed policy, a hosted-access approval is not available for a service monetized through direct fees or related indirect financial benefits. Paid memberships, ads, sponsorship, linked fundraising/cost-recovery requests, affiliates, data sales and promotion of paid offerings cannot be treated as “free” merely because the browser binary is not sold. This does not revoke ordinary internal business use or monetized videos/reviews. A future separately negotiated commercial contract, if any, is outside this approval model.
