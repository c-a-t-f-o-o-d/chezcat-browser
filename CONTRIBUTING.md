# Contributing

This publication preview proposes an initial reports-only policy. Code contributions
and implementation PRs remain closed until separate contribution terms are
approved. Do not submit source archives, binaries, copied website programs or
patches under an assumed inbound license.

Useful reports describe the version/build identity, steps, expected/actual behavior
and a small scenario in your own words. Share only material you may share. Remove
passwords, session cookies/tokens, personal records and private URLs. Arrange terms
before supplying source/test data that needs copying. See SECURITY.md for sensitive
reports; public issues are not confidential.

Reporting does not assign copyright, promise acceptance, require publication of
private modifications or approve arbitrary commercial relicensing. A DCO/sign-off
alone is not a copyright assignment or a substitute for an adequate contribution
grant. Criticism and discussion remain allowed. GitHub platform rights are separate
from the intended CSAPUL grant; source visibility does not promise general
redistribution permission or that platform forks can be disabled.
