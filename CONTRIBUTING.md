# Contributing

ChezCat welcomes ordinary bug reports, compatibility observations and feature suggestions. Submit ordinary issues through the official repository. Use the private security-reporting route for sensitive findings.

## Feedback versus material for inclusion

Non-code suggestions and original explanatory text submitted as feedback may be used as described in CSAPUL Section 8.1. An idea does not give its author an ownership interest in the project, and the project need not implement or pay for it.

Code patches, substantial executable tests, documentation and artwork proposed for inclusion require the [separate Contributor Copyright Assignment](licensing/CONTRIBUTOR_AGREEMENT.md). A minimal reproducer attached to a bug report may be evaluated as evidence, but incorporating its protected code into the product or test suite requires the applicable inclusion rights; calling it “feedback” is not a shortcut.

## Public versus private submissions

Public issues and code pull requests are publicly visible and subject to GitHub's applicable content, forking and inbound-license terms. Rejection does not recall copies or undo platform permissions already granted. Public code submissions are permitted for evaluation; they are not confidential and are not ownership transfers. Do not submit confidential material publicly. Arrange a private route before sending it, and use the security route for sensitive vulnerability details.

GitHub's applicable Terms of Service also describe provider and affiliate processing of public submissions, including certain AI-related uses; the AI-feature account opt-out does not cover every separate public-content grant. Review the current terms before posting and ensure You have authority for those platform uses. This warning discloses the hosting terms, not additional rights granted to every recipient by CSAPUL. Private material requires separate review of the actual account agreement, feature settings and authorized evaluation systems.

## Review and acceptance process

1. Identify the proposed change and its authorship, rights holder, immutable revision or patch digest, and any existing material it includes.

2. Obtain and retain the contributor's explicit evaluation assent under C2 before copying, executing or modifying substantive material beyond independently applicable platform permissions. The submission form or an authenticated written acknowledgement must identify the exact C2 terms/version or digest, immutable submission revision/digest, assenting rights holder or authorized representative, and date. Opening a pull request is not itself recorded C2 assent. Record it before the maintainer or review/CI systems exercise that evaluation permission; do not infer it from running a workflow. Limited bug-reproducer evaluation has the separate permission in CSAPUL 8.1. Public viewing and inbound platform licensing do not replace signed assignment.

3. Review engineering quality and provenance. The maintainer may reject, request changes or accept only a stated portion.

4. Complete Schedule A and obtain signatures from every necessary rights holder or authorized representative. Do not merge substantive code without the executed assignment and express acceptance.

5. Preserve the signed record privately. Record the public credit name/handle and accepted contribution description in CONTRIBUTORS.md and preserve accurate Git authorship.

6. Changes to the accepted scope require an updated record; the agreement is not an unlimited grab of future work.

The following is an acknowledgement template, not an acknowledgement by any actual contributor:

> I have reviewed and agree to the evaluation-only permission in Section C2 of the ChezCat Contributor Copyright Assignment and Patent Agreement, agreement version/text digest [identify exact text], for Submission [immutable revision or patch digest]. I am the identified rights holder or am authorized to grant that permission. I understand that a public submission is public and subject to applicable platform terms. This evaluation acknowledgement does not transfer copyright or accept my work into the project. Copyright assignment, the license-back and the contribution patent grants require the separate executed agreement and countersigned acceptance of Schedule A.

Complete the identifiers before presenting the acknowledgement. Retain the actual response, sender/authority evidence and date; do not store unnecessary personal details in public. A checked box is evidence only to the extent the surrounding process establishes legally sufficient assent and authority. A new material revision must be covered by an appropriate updated record.

The accepted original copyright transfers to Adam Maddox under the signed agreement. Contributors receive the narrow license-back for their own separable work only; it does not grant existing ChezCat code. Contributor patent permission is a separate grant. Contributors keep appropriate historical credit but receive no veto, royalty entitlement, promise of permanent inclusion or automatic official-branch status.

The maintainer must document relevant human authorship, employment/third-party rights and any tool-assisted material before representing that ownership has transferred. A blanket assertion that the project owns every generated line is not sufficient.

For a public fork, applicable GitHub rights remain in effect. Forking is neither an assignment to the maintainer nor an unrestricted license to commercialize the browser. Do not upload credentials, sensitive browsing captures, personal data or material whose owner has not authorized submission.
