# CSAPUL 1.1 changes

CSAPUL 1.1 applies only to releases expressly designated under it. Previously
granted CSAPUL 1.0 rights are not replaced or withdrawn. The browser's product
version is independent of the license version.

Compared with 1.0, this text expressly protects the duration and irrevocability of
base copyright and patent grants, subject to the stated termination rules and
nonwaivable law. Special approvals and branding remain separately scoped.

It clarifies internal users (including owners, partners, temporary workers,
individual contractors, interns and volunteers acting solely for the organization),
while keeping separate affiliates and IT-provider engagements distinct. Ordinary
IT providers accept the terms for their engagements and may factually list ChezCat
among supported software without implying an approved dedicated commercial offering.

A narrow source-only contribution-fork permission supports proposing contributions
on the official hosting platform and retaining historical contribution records;
it does not authorize independent binary distribution, mirrors or hosted services.

New discretionary approvals and prospective withdrawal of special permissions are
addressed expressly. Ordinary withdrawal under the default rule requires at least
30 days after established receipt of written notice; specified serious risks can
justify immediate suspension. Existing base grants and express separate agreements
remain protected. Branding standards address misleading modified builds.

The license describes affirmative offline-capable acceptance, minimal local
receipts, organizational automation, unchanged-text reuse and renewed acceptance
for changed terms. A signed adoption record is additional evidence, not the sole
condition of an otherwise effective grant. The forum wording identifies the
Middle District of Tennessee/Nashville and Dickson County, with mandatory-law and
specified interim-relief exceptions. Qualifying pre-notice reinstatement requests
receive a response within a reasonable time; silence is not reinstatement.

The contributor agreement's separate version 1.1 strengthens the narrow license-back
and downstream patent protections, expressly addresses electronic-transaction
consent, and preserves exact signed scope and private execution records. Publishing
a blank form transfers no rights and does not amend already-executed agreements.
