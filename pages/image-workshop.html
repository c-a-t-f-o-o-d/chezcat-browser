Where support stops

New: responsive images, fit/crop, SVG effects, TGA and PCX

SVG is currently a static image subset. Clipping paths and alpha/luminance masks are supported. Text, filters, patterns, nested images, nested viewports, animation and complex CSS are not implemented. Unsupported rendering features produce a resource error. SVG images cannot execute scripts or fetch other resources.

AVIF, HEIF/HEIC and JPEG XL are not implemented. TIFF displays its first page; BigTIFF and several specialized TIFF modes remain unsupported. Color profiles and HDR display are not implemented. Video playback and modern video codecs remain separate work.
