Put the page to work

Click a field and type. Tab and Shift+Tab move between controls; Space toggles a focused choice. Labels can be clicked too.

To submit, use the Forms link in the local HTTP or HTTPS demo. The server echoes the values it receives.

A small GET form

Click to open the dropdown. Arrow keys move through choices; Enter selects and Esc cancels. Reset restores HTML and CSS pages.

Independent controls

These are outside the form. The first joins it through its form attribute.

Text/search/tel inputs, single dropdowns, hidden values, checkboxes, radio buttons, submit and reset are supported. URL-encoded POST forms and basic scripted buttons also work; try the POST workshop. Multiple-selection lists, passwords, upload controls, multi-line editing and full validation are later work. Unsupported named controls stop submission with an explanation.
